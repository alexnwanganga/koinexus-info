Koinexus Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 07/27/26

Koinexus ("Koinexus," "we," "us," or "our") is a mobile platform for musicians, worship teams, bands, music directors, and organizations to connect, post and apply for gigs, coordinate workspaces, exchange messages, share setlists and files, manage calendars, and receive notifications.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. This document is a practical draft based on the current app functionality and should be reviewed by legal counsel before publication.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create or use an account, we may collect:

  • Email address and authentication information.
  • Account type, such as musician or organization.
  • Name, username, profile summary, bio, location, availability, experience, genres, instruments, instrument badge, profile photo, and cover image.
  • Onboarding selections and profile settings.

Gig, Application, Invite, and Workspace Information

When you use gig and collaboration features, we may collect:

  • Gig listings created by organization accounts, including title, organization name, location, dates, times, compensation details, role or instrument needs, descriptions, requirements, responsibilities, tags, and rehearsal information.
  • Gig saves, applications, application answers, availability information, application status, acceptances, rejections, and direct gig invites.
  • Workspace membership, roles, setlists, songs, readiness statuses, notes, calendar events, rehearsal votes, and personal availability blocks.
  • Reviews, collaborator history, skills, and gig history added to profiles.

Messages and Shared Content

Koinexus includes direct and team messaging. We may collect and store:

  • Message text.
  • Message links and link metadata.
  • Uploaded message attachments.
  • Workspace files, including audio files uploaded for setlists or rehearsal use.
  • Metadata about files, such as file name, file type, size, uploader, workspace, song, and upload time.

Notifications and Device Information

If you enable notifications, we may collect:

  • Expo push notification tokens.
  • Notification records, read status, delivery status, and notification interaction data.
  • Limited app presence information used to avoid sending push notifications while you are active in the app.

Local Device Storage

The app may store some information locally on your device, including:

  • Authentication session data.
  • Draft gig posts or temporary UI state.
  • Locally cached audio files so playback can work more reliably after the first download.
  • Legacy or backup notification read-state information.

Technical Information

We may collect basic technical information needed to operate and secure the app, such as request logs, error logs, timestamps, and information generated by Supabase, Expo, and related infrastructure.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage accounts.
  • Build and display musician and organization profiles.
  • Help users search for and connect with other musicians or organizations.
  • Allow organizations to post gigs and review, accept, reject, or invite musicians.
  • Allow musicians to save gigs, apply for gigs, accept or decline invites, and join workspaces.
  • Power workspaces, setlists, file sharing, audio playback, notes, calendars, rehearsal voting, and team chats.
  • Send in-app and push notifications about messages, invitations, applications, calendar events, files, connections, and related activity.
  • Sync notification read status and other account data across devices.
  • Maintain app security, debug issues, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. How Information Is Shared

We share information in the ways necessary for the app to work:

  • Public and searchable profile information may be visible to authenticated users.
  • Gig listings may be visible to users who can browse gigs.
  • Applications are visible to the organization that posted the gig.
  • Application rejections and invite decisions may generate notifications to affected users.
  • Workspace content is visible to workspace members.
  • Direct messages are visible to conversation participants.
  • Team chats, setlists, files, notes, calendar events, and votes are visible to workspace members.
  • Profile images and other media may be stored in Supabase Storage and displayed in the app according to the app's access rules.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Service Providers

We use third-party service providers to operate the app, including:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, file storage, realtime updates, and backend functions.
  • Expo services for push notifications and mobile app infrastructure.
  • Apple and Google platform services where applicable for app distribution, device permissions, and push notification delivery.

These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy practices.

5. User Content

You are responsible for the content you add to Koinexus, including profile details, gig listings, applications, messages, links, audio files, notes, and other uploads. Do not upload content that you do not have permission to use or share.

6. Audio Files and Media

Workspace members may upload audio files and related setlist content. The app may download audio files to local device cache or storage for playback and pitch/transpose features. Cached files may remain on the device until cleared by the app, deleted by the user, removed by the operating system, or invalidated after the original file changes or is removed.

7. Account Deletion

You may delete your account in the app from account settings. Account deletion is designed to remove your account, profile data, notification tokens, notifications, calendar availability, gig applications, gig saves, gig invites, profile data, workspace memberships, and content you created where supported by the current system.

Some information may remain where deletion would affect another user's legitimate records, where content has already been shared with other users, where required for security or legal compliance, or where retained in backups for a limited period.

8. Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the app, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we may delete, anonymize, or archive it according to our operational practices.

9. Your Choices

Depending on your account and location, you may be able to:

  • Access and update your profile information in the app.
  • Change notification settings through your device settings.
  • Delete content you created where the app provides deletion controls.
  • Delete your account from account settings.
  • Contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or other privacy rights available under applicable law.

10. Children's Privacy

Koinexus is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11. Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, including Supabase authentication, row-level access controls, and scoped storage access rules. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Regional Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict certain uses of your personal information. You may also have the right to appeal a decision about a privacy request. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will provide notice through the app or by other appropriate means. The updated policy will be effective when posted unless stated otherwise.
